# Session sweep research

M5 bid candles, fixed UTC windows and weekday entries. London requires all 84 Asian M5 bars, a wick beyond that range and same-bar close back inside, followed by a close through the prior confirmed five-bar pivot. New York sweeps the preceding hour, then forms a directionally aligned three-candle FVG and retests/reclaims it before entry. The signal close must agree with completed UTC H4 EMA50 and daily EMA20. Entries use the next contiguous bar open, stops buffer the sweep extreme by 0.2 ATR14, and open trades flatten by the window close. Incomplete windows are excluded. The NY hour is 10:00 EDT in summer and 09:00 EST in winter.

Two chronological OOS windows cover the final 40% of observed dates. The preceding fifth selects 2.5R or 3R using stressed mean R with priority for 10+ trades. Baseline uses historical spread with causal fallback, two points slippage per side and $7 round-trip commission per lot (or one point fallback); stress uses double spread, five points and 1.5x commission. Sizing is 1% compounded equity. Results and fold-specific selections are in session_walkforward.csv. Run python session_walkforward.py from this worktree.
